Acoustic Studio Drum Samples
============================
Source: The Open Source Drum Kit (Real Music Media)
Repository: https://github.com/crabacus/the-open-source-drumkit
License: Public Domain

Close-mic'd studio acoustic recordings converted to 44.1 kHz 16-bit mono WAV
and shaped in real time by Drum Sheesh's pure-Kotlin room ambience, punch,
and tone DSP engine (:core ProceduralDrumSynthesizer.processWavWithProfile).
